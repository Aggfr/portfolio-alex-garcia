About

Designing Solutions with a Focus on Human Experience

As a Product Design Engineering, I am deeply passionate about creating digital experiences that resonate with users on a fundamental level. My approach is rooted in anthropology and sociology, allowing me to delve into the intricate tapestry of human behavior and cultural nuances that shape our interactions with technology.

While my professional life revolves around understanding human behavior, my personal passions are deeply connected to nature and adventure. When I'm not designing, you'll find me exploring the volcanic landscapes of Lanzarote with my loyal dog. The island's unique environment also fuels my love for spearfishing and surfing.
